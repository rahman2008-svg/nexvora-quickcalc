Understanding Percentage Mathematics

Standard Percentage (X% of Y)

Calculates the proportional fraction of a quantity: P = (X / 100) × Y.

Ratio to Percentage

Determines what proportion X represents out of whole Y: P = (X / Y) × 100.

Percentage Increase

Measures proportional growth between initial X and final Y: ((Y - X) / X) × 100.

Percentage Decrease

Measures proportional drop from original X to lower Y: ((X - Y) / X) × 100.
