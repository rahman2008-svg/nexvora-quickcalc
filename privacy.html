Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • NexVora Labs Ofc

1. Core Principle: 100% Client-Side Processing

At NexVora Labs Ofc, your privacy is our primary engineering requirement. NexVora QuickCalc operates completely within your web browser. When you enter numbers, perform calculations, compute percentages, or determine discounts, none of your mathematical expressions, results, or data are transmitted to any remote web server or third-party database.

2. LocalStorage & Persistent Preferences

The application uses your browser's standard localStorage mechanism strictly for the following local convenience features:

  • Calculation History: Up to 50 recent calculations are saved locally to allow you to review and reuse previous outputs. You can permanently delete this history at any time using the "Clear History" button.
  • Theme Preference: Remembers your preference between Dark Mode and Light Mode.
  • Language Preference: Remembers whether you selected English or বাংলা (Bengali).

This data is stored solely inside your browser profile on your device and is never shared, synced, or sold.

3. Clipboard Access

NexVora QuickCalc includes a "Copy" function that copies calculation results to your device clipboard upon your explicit click. The application only writes text to the clipboard when you trigger the copy action; it never reads or monitors your clipboard content.

4. Service Worker & Offline Caching

NexVora QuickCalc registers a Service Worker to cache static site assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG icons) so that the application loads instantly and operates without internet connectivity. The Service Worker does not cache private user calculation data or personally identifiable information.

5. Future Monetization & Third-Party Advertising Disclosure

In version 1.0.0, all advertisements are completely disabled. If in future updates NexVora Labs Ofc partners with certified advertising networks (such as Google AdSense), those third-party services may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads according to their respective privacy policies. This privacy policy will be updated transparently prior to any such integration. We do not claim zero data collection in the event third-party services are subsequently enabled.
